## WHAT IS GII CONNECT?

### Identity
- **Name:** GII Connect
- **Type:** For-profit EdTech startup (separate from GII 501c3)
- **Status:** Trial launch phase
- **Core Philosophy:** "Everyone builds edtech for connected classrooms. We built it for the classrooms that actually exist."

### The Platform
- **Offline-First:** Works without internet
- **Multilingual:** Krio, Mende, Temne, Luo, Swahili
- **Mobile-First:** Works on basic smartphones
- **AI-Powered:** Personalized learning
- **Battle-Tested:** 3,348 learners across 7 countries

### Institutional Validation
- **St. Andrew's Secondary:** Made ICT MANDATORY (~500 students)
- **United Brethren in Christ:** Made ICT MANDATORY (~500 students)
- **What This Means:** Schools chose it without payment = real proof

### Revenue Streams
1. **NGO White-Label Licensing:** $2-5K setup + $4-8/learner/year
2. **Private School Licensing:** $1.2-2.3/student (Kenya)
3. **Government Deployments:** Named tech vendor in grants
4. **Opportunity Hub:** Placement fees from employers
5. **Sponsorships:** Telco/bank CSR funding

- **Gross Margin:** 90%+
- **Revenue Target:** $50K-$370K by month 18
- **Break-Even:** ~$160K ARR
