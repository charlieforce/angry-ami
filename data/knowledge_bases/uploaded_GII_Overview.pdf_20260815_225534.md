Who We Are
Global Impact Innovators (GII) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to empowering African entrepreneurs, particularly women
and those in rural areas, by providing free, accessible tech startup and entrepreneurship training. We operate in Sierra
Leone and Kenya, leveraging mobile technology and local language support to foster sustainable economic growth and
innovation across the continent.
Our Vision
A thriving, inclusive tech ecosystem across Africa where women and young entrepreneurs in underserved communities
have equal access to education, mentorship, and opportunities to create positive change and sustainable innovation.
Our Global Impact
Since launching operations in January 2025, GII has reached learners across Africa with transformative tech and
entrepreneurship training:
 3,348+ total learners across 7 countries | 2,080+ program completions | 1,268 active participants
 Sierra Leone operations: 720 students across 12 schools in Bo District (Term 2, April 2026)
 Mandatory ICT adoption at two institutions: St. Andrew's Secondary School (500 students) and United Brethren in
Christ SSS (500 students)—the first mandatory institutional adoptions in Sierra Leone
 Bo Cohort 2 Entrepreneurship: 30 participants with 76.7% female representation
 Verified partnerships with Ministry of Basic Education Bo, ForestLand Foundation, and community organizations
Our Programs
ICT & Digital Literacy: Comprehensive tech and digital literacy programs delivered to secondary schools and
communities, building foundational tech skills for students in underserved areas.
Entrepreneurship Training: Startup fundamentals, business planning, pitch preparation, and peer mentorship for
aspiring African founders looking to scale their ideas.
Workforce Development: Industry-aligned skills training to prepare youth for employment, freelancing, and tech
career pathways.
AI & STEM Education: Cutting-edge education in artificial intelligence, coding, and STEM disciplines tailored to
African contexts.
Where We Operate
Our primary operations are centered in Sierra Leone (Bo District headquarters) and Kenya (Nairobi base), with
partnerships extending across 7 African countries. We're expanding our reach through strategic partnerships including an
upcoming bootcamp at the Kakuma refugee camp in partnership with AReL (African Refugee Leadership Initiative).
Global Impact Innovators
Flagship Initiatives
Bo Term 2 ICT Program (April 2026)
Our flagship initiative reached 720 students across 12 schools in Bo District, Sierra Leone. This structured program
introduces digital literacy, coding basics, and tech entrepreneurship to secondary students, with curriculum delivered in
English and Krio for accessibility.
Mandatory School Adoption
A historic milestone for African tech education: two institutions have integrated GII's curriculum as mandatory coursework.
St. Andrew's Secondary School and United Brethren in Christ SSS now require all students to complete GII training,
reaching 1,000+ learners across these institutions alone.
GII Connect: EdTech Platform
Our complementary for-profit platform delivers online courses in tech entrepreneurship, PM, freelancing, coding, and AI.
GII Connect accelerates our reach beyond geographic limitations and creates pathways for global learners to access
African-designed, culturally-relevant tech education.
Leadership
GII is Co-Founded and led by Charles Bond Kebbi (Managing Director), a U.S. Army veteran, Microsoft Teams Senior
